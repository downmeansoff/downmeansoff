I am an **AI Engineer / Product Engineer at Fortune Tavern Ltd (UK)** building agentic systems, AI-assisted business automation, production backends, and distributed infrastructure.

My focus is not adding a chatbot to an existing process. I turn business operations into reliable software systems with explicit states, tools, data contracts, validation, approval gates, observability, testing, and recovery paths.

## AgentOps workflow

```text
Business goal
    ↓
Scoped architecture & acceptance criteria
    ↓
Specialized coding agents in isolated branches
    ↓
Tests, security review, CI and staging validation
    ↓
Human-controlled production decision
```

I use coding agents as an engineering force multiplier, not as an uncontrolled replacement for engineering ownership. Agents can analyze, implement, test, and review; production mutations, architecture, risk boundaries, and release decisions remain human-controlled.

---

## Engineering principles

- Model business workflows as explicit states, tools, permissions, and transitions
- Prefer structured outputs and validation over unrestricted model text
- Design critical operations for idempotency, retries, and graceful degradation
- Measure business outcomes, not just model quality
- Treat tests, observability, auditability, rollback, and recovery as product features
- Keep AI agents fast in development and strictly gated away from uncontrolled production access
